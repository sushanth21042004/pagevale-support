Privacy Policy

Private by design.

PageVale is built to process documents on your device. We do not operate a document-processing server, require an account, or track how you use the app.

Effective October 19, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PageVale, provided by Sushanth Reddy (“PageVale,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use PageVale on iPhone, iPad, or Mac, visit this website, or contact support.

2. Information we collect

The app does not transmit your documents or usage analytics to us. It does not require a PageVale account and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or telemetry SDKs.

Through normal app use, we do not receive your document contents, filenames, document passwords, signatures, OCR results, or processing history. Information you voluntarily submit to support is handled separately as described below.

3. On-device document processing

PDF and image operations are performed on your device using Apple system frameworks and PageVale’s local processing tools. You choose the input files and where finished copies are saved. PageVale does not silently overwrite original files.

Files selected from iCloud Drive or another storage provider are made available through Apple’s system file picker. Your relationship with that storage provider is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Features that use a network connection

The Webpage to PDF feature connects to the webpage address you explicitly enter so the requested page can be loaded and converted. That website may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, and may use its own cookies or technologies under its privacy policy. PageVale does not use this feature to track you.

5. Purchases

Optional PageVale Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Apple processes purchase information according to Apple’s privacy policy. The app reads Apple-provided entitlement information to unlock purchased features. Apple may provide us with App Store sales and reporting information.

6. Local information and retention

PageVale uses a private temporary workspace on your device for copies of imported documents and generated results. These copies may remain until the app or operating system clears them; they are not uploaded to us. Save finished results to a location you choose before relying on them as permanent copies.

Preferences and other app state may be stored locally. Exported documents remain where you saved them until you remove them. Removing the app does not necessarily remove exported files, cloud copies, or backups; manage these separately in Files, Finder, and your storage-provider settings.

7. Security

PageVale uses Apple’s sandboxing and system file-access controls. No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, so you should protect your device, keep your operating system updated, and store sensitive files only in locations you trust.

8. This website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies to these pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and handles hosting and account information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you open a support issue, we can see your GitHub username and any information you submit. Issues and replies are public and may be indexed by search engines. We use this information to respond to requests and troubleshoot problems. Do not submit private documents, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Support discussions remain available until removed through GitHub’s controls. You can edit your own submissions or request removal of content we control. Copies held by GitHub, search engines, or other parties are subject to their own policies. We do not sell support information or use it for advertising.

9. Children’s privacy

PageVale is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through support. If such information has been submitted, contact us without repeating the sensitive information so we can address it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PageVale changes or when legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact and privacy requests

Questions about this policy or PageVale can be submitted through the official PageVale support page. Do not include confidential documents, document passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information in a public support request.
